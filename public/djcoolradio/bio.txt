Many different styles and flavors spanning generations from 1939 to today. Sukhi has it all — from rock to house to dubstep and back again, R&B old and new, hip hop, garage, soul, funk, breaks, disco, and traditional music from cultures around the world.

Having toured internationally and played in Australia, Mexico, the U.S.A. and Canada, Sukhi brings a unique approach to musical flow, heavily influenced by cultural music and decades of experience behind the decks.

Sukhi recently debuted his first album, “My Heart Was Full,” available wherever you stream your music.

A veteran of the industry with the skills to match, DJ Sukhi Singh has been rockin’ turntablism and controllerism since 1994. Whether it’s a party, club, radio show, festival, private event or corporate event, Sukhi brings the energy and musical range to move the room.

As a multi-instrument musician, Sukhi approaches the decks as more than simply mixing two songs together. He treats the setup like an instrument — producing beats, reshaping tracks and creating music on the fly.

“Music is to touch a soul. That is a gift and a responsibility.”
— Sukhi Singh
